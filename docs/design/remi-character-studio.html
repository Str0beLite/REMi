What changed from the ChatGPT spec

  • Actions follow the current plan

    The spec's 15 poses are the data-center incident pipeline. REMi now chats, runs and proposes tools, and trains overnight. Incidents are extension 1, so ingest, gather and page can come back then on the same rig.

  • Sleep is the signature moment

    The dream bubble draws the live loss curve from the sleep_event stream, so "agents that sleep on it" is something the audience can watch.

  • Adapters are shirt patches

    Each adopted LoRA version appears as a numbered patch. Rollback flips it back, so adapter history shows on the character itself.

  • SVG rig with real parenting

    Ears are children of the head group, as the spec requires. They also trail the head by about 90 ms and counter-swing a little, so they read as soft ears instead of discs glued on.

  • A small-size mark

    At 24 to 48 px the rig drops body, paws and brows, enlarges the eyes and thickens the line. Status shows in the face: closed eyes for asleep, flat eyes and drained color for stopped.

  • Dark-mode tail fix

    The spec draws the tail as a plum line, which nearly vanishes on its own dark surface. Here it is an outlined tube in fur color, so it reads on every stage.

  • Kept from the spec

    Three-second finite actions, no idle bouncing (only a blink), happy face only after a verified outcome, and reduced motion jumps straight to each end pose.

  • Squash and anticipation

    Big moves start with a small squash in the opposite direction and settle with a little overshoot, so a mention or a wake-up has weight at small sizes.
